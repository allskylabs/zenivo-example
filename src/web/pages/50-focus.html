Exposure while focusing

Auto-exposure pauses in focus mode so the score stays comparable. If the light changed, meter once. Short exposures refresh faster and blur less.

How to focus

  • Aim at a bright star near the centre (or a distant light by day), then press Start focus mode.
  • Move the lens until the star size drops or the sharpness score rises; the Best marker shows the sharpest position.
  • Press Finish & save to keep the lens position and leave focus mode.
